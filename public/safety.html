Safety Guidelines

  • RandomMeet is intended for adults aged 18 and over.
  • Do not record, share, or publish another person’s video, voice, or personal information without their consent.
  • Never share your address, identity documents, bank details, passwords, or live location.
  • If you experience harassment, fraud, or threats, end the chat and leave immediately.

Important: this beta has no human moderation team. The report button is not connected to case storage or a moderation workflow. Do not treat this beta as a fully moderated social platform.
