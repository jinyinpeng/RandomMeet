SAFETY FIRST

Safety Guidelines

RandomMeet is intended only for people aged 18 or older. The age prompt is self-attestation, not identity or age verification.

  • Never share your address, identity documents, bank details, passwords, or live location.
  • Do not record, share, or publish another person's video, voice, or personal information without consent.
  • End the chat immediately if you experience harassment, unwanted sexual content, threats, scams, or pressure to share private information.
  • Use Report to submit a safety reason; submitting ends the chat. Reports currently enter a temporary in-memory queue and may be lost on restart. This beta does not yet have staffed moderation or durable report storage.
  • Use Block & leave to end the current chat and prevent matching with that browser identifier from this browser. This is not a guaranteed identity-level ban and can be bypassed by clearing browser data or using another browser/device.
  • Do not use the service for illegal activity, threats, stalking, exploitation, or to contact minors.

If someone is in immediate danger

Leave the chat and contact local emergency services. RandomMeet is not an emergency response service.

Before broad public launch, a reliable, monitored report-handling process and durable storage must be in place. No user should rely on this beta as a fully moderated platform.
