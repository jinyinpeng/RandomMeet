Eligibility

You may use RandomMeet only if you are at least 18 years old and legally permitted to use the service where you are located. The age confirmation is self-declared and does not verify your age.

Acceptable use

You must not use the service to harass, threaten, stalk, exploit, defraud, impersonate, or unlawfully record another person. Do not share or solicit sexual content involving minors. Do not use the service for illegal activity or to evade applicable law.

Random connections and user conduct

Participants are strangers. Gender and nationality are self-reported and unverified. RandomMeet does not guarantee a user's identity, conduct, availability, or the suitability of a match. You are responsible for deciding whether to continue a conversation and should not share sensitive personal information.

Reports and blocks

Report and block controls are provided as beta safety tools. Reports are currently held in temporary in-memory storage and may be lost when the service restarts; there is not yet a staffed moderation team or a durable case-management process. Blocking applies to a browser-generated identifier and is not an identity-level ban.

Availability and beta status

The service is provided as a beta and may be changed, interrupted, or unavailable. The project has not undergone an independent security audit. These draft terms do not limit rights that cannot legally be limited under applicable law.
