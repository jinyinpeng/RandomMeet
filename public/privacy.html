Privacy Notice (Beta)

This beta requests access to your camera and microphone through your browser and uses WebRTC to attempt a direct connection between users’ devices. The server relays connection negotiation data and does not intentionally record media. However, another person could record you using an external device, and this project has not undergone an independent security audit.

Current data practices

Matching uses temporary session identifiers, chat mode, and the preferences you select. Your self-reported gender and nationality are stored in your browser’s local storage and sent to the server for matching. There is no account system, persistent server-side profile, persistent block list, or data-deletion portal. The report button only displays a notice; reports are not saved or sent to moderators.

Do not share your full name, home address, identity documents, financial information, or other sensitive information. Before a public launch, this project needs a formal privacy policy, contact details, data retention and deletion processes, and a review of applicable laws.
