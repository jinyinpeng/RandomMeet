Privacy Notice

RandomMeet is a beta service for adult-to-adult random video and voice chat. This notice describes the current implementation. It is not a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific legal review. The operator's contact details and a durable process for privacy requests must be added before a public launch.

Information processed

  • Camera and microphone: your browser asks permission before local media capture. Media is exchanged using WebRTC, preferably directly between devices. When a TURN relay is configured, media may pass through that relay. RandomMeet's application server does not intentionally record audio or video. Other users can still capture what they see or hear using separate equipment or software.
  • Matching profile: gender and nationality are self-reported. They are stored in this browser's local storage and sent with matching preferences to the application server.
  • Temporary connection data: the server processes socket identifiers, temporary session identifiers, signaling messages, and connection state to establish and end chats.
  • Block list and recent matches: browser-generated identifiers for blocked users and recent matches are stored in local storage and sent with matching requests to avoid those users. Clearing this site's browser data removes these local lists.
  • Safety reports: when you submit a report, the server stores the selected reason, report timestamp, random browser identifiers, and session identifier in a bounded in-memory queue. The queue is not durable and may be lost when the service restarts or redeploys. No media recording is attached. This beta does not yet provide a staffed moderation service.
  • Operational logs: the hosting provider may process request and service logs under its own policies. Do not assume the service is anonymous or that network metadata is never processed.

Retention, access, and deletion

Profile, blocked-user, and recent-match data are stored in your browser; you can clear RandomMeet's site data in your browser settings. The current beta has no account system or self-service data export/deletion portal. Safety reports are volatile in-memory records rather than durable records. Before broad public launch, the operator must establish appropriate retention, access controls, deletion procedures, a privacy contact, and a reliable report-storage and response process.

Security and third parties

The service uses HTTPS on its public Render deployment and applies browser security headers. It has not undergone an independent security audit. WebRTC direct connections can expose network information to the other participant in some configurations; a TURN relay may change the network path but does not make a conversation risk-free. Hosting, signaling, and any configured STUN/TURN providers may process technical data according to their own terms.

Your choices and safety

You can decline camera or microphone permissions and leave any chat. Do not share your home address, identity documents, financial details, passwords, or live location. Do not use RandomMeet if you are under 18. Do not assume another participant's identity, age, gender, or nationality has been verified.

Contact and legal requests

Before public launch, this page must identify the service operator and provide a monitored privacy contact, including a process for applicable access, correction, objection, and deletion requests. If you need help now, use the hosting project's published contact route; do not send sensitive personal data in a public issue.
